# Strategic Roadmap: AI-Guardian MVP

## Phase 1: Institutional Foundation (Proof of Minimization)
**Objective:** Enable Enterprises to prove their AI data is "Clean."
- **Deliverable:** "Minimization Proxy" for CSV/JSON datasets.
- **Key Metric:** 0% PII leakage in exported training sets.
- **Market Segment:** Pharma and Data-Brokers.

## Phase 2: Operational Trust (Proof of Deletion)
**Objective:** Solve the "Right to be Forgotten" for large-scale training.
- **Deliverable:** Automated `ShredProof` generation after batch processing.
- **Key Metric:** Audit-ready cryptographic proof of destruction.
- **Market Segment:** Finance and Legal Tech.

## Phase 3: Consumer Empowerment (Privacy-First Prompting)
**Objective:** Personal AI protection for the mass market.
- **Deliverable:** Web-plugin or Browser-based "Prompt Sanitizer."
- **Key Metric:** Number of PII data points blocked per prompt.
- **Market Segment:** Individual Pro-Users.

## Phase 4: Full Autonomy (Verifiable Inference)
**Objective:** Hardware-enforced AI agents.
- **Deliverable:** Edge-Guardian for IoT and Robotics.
- **Key Metric:** Latency vs. Security trade-off optimization.
- **Market Segment:** Industrial IoT and Automotive.
