# miTch Messaging Rules

Stand: 2026-05-21

Use these rules when editing the landing page, pitch copy, or demo intro.

## Primary message

- Lead with the action: `Prove only what is needed.`
- Explain the mechanism: selective disclosure.
- Show the consequence: less data leaves the wallet.
- End with the proof: auditable, decision-bound flow.

## What to say

- `miTch sits between the wallet and the verifier.`
- `The verifier gets the minimum proof required.`
- `Raw data stays out of the request path.`
- `The disclosure decision is auditable.`
- `Requested, allowed, withheld` are the three visible states.

## What not to say

- Do not say `we block all tracking`.
- Do not say `no one can see anything`.
- Do not say `full anonymity`.
- Do not say `privacy score` unless the page is explicitly about audit scanning.
- Do not claim browser-wide enforcement if the demo is wallet-scoped.

## Stronger replacements

- Replace `privacy firewall` with `identity firewall` when the point is tracker visibility.
- Replace `privacy audit` with `visibility scan` or `disclosure audit` when the point is pre-proof inspection.
- Replace `full audit trail` with `decision evidence` unless the page links the actual log model.
- Replace `safe` with a concrete property like `fail-closed`, `PII-minimal`, or `auditable`.

## Page structure rule

Every major section should answer one of these:

1. What problem does miTch solve?
2. What exactly does it do?
3. What evidence shows it is real?
4. Who needs it?
5. What should the user do next?

## Conversion rule

The page should push one action only:

- Open the live demo

Secondary actions are allowed, but they must not compete with the demo CTA.
