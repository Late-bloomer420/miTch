# miTch LinkedIn Campaign Series

## Campaign frame

Theme: Privacy by proof, not by promise.
Audience: product leaders, compliance teams, security teams, public-sector buyers.

## Post 1

**Hook:** Most identity systems ask for too much.

**Body:**  
miTch changes the default. A verifier asks a question. The wallet answers with the minimum proof required. No raw data dump. No unnecessary identifiers. No blind trust.

**Close:**  
That is what a privacy firewall looks like in practice.

**CTA:** See the demo.

## Post 2

**Hook:** Privacy is not a settings page.

**Body:**  
If a user has to hunt for control, the system already failed. miTch makes control part of the transaction itself: requested, allowed, withheld. Every disclosure is explicit. Every decision is auditable.

**Close:**  
Privacy should be enforced in the flow, not explained after the fact.

**CTA:** Request a pilot.

## Post 3

**Hook:** Compliance should reduce exposure, not create it.

**Body:**  
With miTch, the verifier gets a cryptographic proof instead of a copy of the underlying data. That means less liability, less retention risk, and a cleaner audit story.

**Close:**  
Minimization is not a feature. It is the architecture.

**CTA:** Explore the wallet flow.

## Post 4

**Hook:** The best identity system is the one that forgets.

**Body:**  
miTch is built around fail-closed policy and crypto-shredding. When the purpose is complete, the system does not keep collecting, copying, or linking.

**Close:**  
If it does not need to persist, it should not persist.

**CTA:** See the live demo.

## Post 5

**Hook:** The next identity layer should be invisible when it works.

**Body:**  
Users should not feel like they are handing over their whole life just to prove one thing. miTch keeps the proof narrow, the policy strict, and the audit trail clear.

**Close:**  
That is the practical future of digital identity.
