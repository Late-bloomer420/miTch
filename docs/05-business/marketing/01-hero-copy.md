# miTch Hero Copy

## Primary headline
Prove only what is needed.

## Supporting line
miTch is the privacy firewall for identity proofs. Verifiers get the minimum answer they need. Raw data stays out.

## Short subhead options
1. Selective disclosure. Fail-closed policy. Full audit trail.
2. Prove age, status, eligibility, or role without over-sharing.
3. The Forgetting Layer for modern identity flows.

## CTA options
- See the live demo
- Request a pilot
- Explore the wallet flow

## Hero message structure
- Lead with the claim, not the category.
- Show the benefit in one sentence.
- Make the privacy mechanism visible in the product.

## Visual cue
Use a wallet control surface with three states: requested, allowed, withheld.
